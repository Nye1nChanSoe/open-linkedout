About the Company

GLOBAL TAX FREE is a tax refund specialist that provides VAT refund services for international tourists.

We are currently recruiting for our Japanese subsidiary. Please see below for further details.

Position Overview

We are seeking a Software Developer to support both system development and reliable operations for our Japanese subsidiary.

This role will translate local business and technical requirements into system solutions and cover a broad range of responsibilities, from integration with external partners and terminal devices to collaboration with the development and deployment teams at our Headquarters in Korea.


Key Responsibilities

  • Implement and modify integration interfaces for external partners and terminal devices.
  • Respond to external technical inquiries and support the preparation of API specifications.
  • Provide Level 2 operational support for local systems and apply the deployment processes established by Headquarters in Korea.


Required Qualifications and Competencies

  • Mid-level candidate with 3–6 years of relevant professional experience.
  • Experience in backend development using Java, Spring Boot, or similar technologies, as well as API integration.
  • Proficiency in Oracle Database, SQL, and data processing.
  • Professional proficiency in both Korean and Japanese, spoken and written, is mandatory, as this position requires regular communication with Headquarters in Korea.


Application and Interview Information

  • Only applicants who pass the document screening stage will be contacted individually regarding the interview schedule.
