1. Job Detail Page Structure and Terms I used here
--------------------------------------------------

  LinkedIn /jobs/view/:id
  |
  +-- [data-testid="job-details-header"]                <- "the header"
  |       Jitta
  |       Software Engineer, Investment Algorithms
  |       Bangkok - 1 week ago - Over 100 applicants
  |       Hybrid
  |       Full-time
  |       Apply   Save
  |
  +-- [id^="JobDetails_AboutTheJob_"]                  <- "the description slot"
          <h2>About the job</h2>                       <- LinkedIn's label for the slot
          <span data-testid="expandable-text-box">     <- the employer's actual ad
             SUMMARY ...
             JOB AREAS ...
             KEY RESPONSIBILITIES ...
          ... more                                     <- the expand control (but don't need to click)
          Benefits found in job post                   <- a LinkedIn-generated panel


2. Column Mapping
------------------

  header_text            the header block, as visible text
  workplace_type         parsed OUT OF header_text
  employment_type        parsed OUT OF header_text
  applicant_count        parsed OUT OF header_text

  description_text       the description slot, as visible text
  description_html       the description slot, as markup


Our parser ignores the useless text blocks with 
IGNORED_BLOCK_TEXTS
IGNORED_HEADINGS


"Job description" means two different things depending on the level:

  (a) THE WHOLE SLOT
    description_text / description_html. Everything under "About the job".
  (b) A SECTION INSIDE IT
    12 of the 91 corpus jobs open their ad with a heading
    that literally reads "Job Description", alongside
    "Role Overview" and "Job Summary". The parser maps all
    of those to sectionType "overview".

So (b) lives inside (a). A section never comes from the header.
